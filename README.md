# TheDenial : Gameplay Demonstration

**Link** : https://youtube.com/shorts/dzi-DSH8ntk?feature=share

Here's the gameplay demonstration video for our VR videogame **TheDenial**, developed on Unreal Engine 5.
Step into the shoes of Danny, a bitter and worn-out teacher... Keep a sharp eye and target unscrupulous students in the shortest possible time to earn points. But be careful not to make a mistake, or you'll lose your hard-earned points !
